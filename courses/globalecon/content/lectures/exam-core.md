## The ten traps

1. **Price off the MR line.** P always comes from demand: 339 − 96 = 243, not 147.
2. **Cartel MR.** With q each, P = 339 − 2q, so MR = 339 − **4**q, not 2q.
3. **Bertrand profit is zero**, not "half the monopoly profit". P = MC.
4. **Cournot treats the rival's q as a constant** when differentiating: MR_G = 339 − 2q_G − q_U.
5. **Mixed p uses the opponent's payoffs.**
6. **"Total Value" in the table is P × Q**, not consumer surplus.
7. **Saving has a level effect, not a growth effect.** Long-run growth of y per worker is unchanged.
8. **War on capital → faster growth; war on workers → output per worker falls afterwards.**
9. **Golden Rule is MPK = δ**, not MPK = 0 and not "the highest k". Too much capital lowers consumption.
10. **δ + n + g add**, because new workers do not get the technology boost.
