## How Parts 3 and 4 work

Part 3 matches **grape variety ↔ country**. Part 4 matches **designation ↔ country**, or
designation ↔ grape. Every pool has spare answers, so you cannot get the last pair by
elimination.

The trap is a grape grown in more than one country on the slides. Learn these as pairs:

| Grape | Signature country | Also on the slides |
|---|---|---|
| Malbec | **Argentina** | France (Cahors) |
| Tannat | **Uruguay** | France (Madiran), Peru |
| Carménère | **Chile** | France (as the extinct Grand Vidure) |
| Zinfandel | **USA** | = Primitivo (Puglia, Italy), from Croatia |
| Torrontés | **Argentina** | believed to come from Galicia, Spain |
| Saperavi | **Georgia** | Ukraine |
| Pinotage | **South Africa** | — |
| Sauvignon Blanc | **New Zealand** (42%) | everywhere |
| Shiraz | **Australia** (most planted) | = Syrah (Rhône) |

## How to learn it

Read one row across, cover it, and say the country from the grape. Then do the reverse: say
the grapes and designations from the country. The quiz modes "Final exam part 3" and "Final
exam part 4" build new five-item matches from this table every run.
