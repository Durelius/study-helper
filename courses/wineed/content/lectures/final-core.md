## 1. The pairing polygon

The AIS chart (*scheda grafica di abbinamento*) scores the dish in **red** and the wine in
**blue**, each from 0 to 10: imperceivable 0-2, slightly 2-4, moderately 4-6, perceivable 6-8,
highly perceivable 8-10. Each wine axis faces the food it has to answer:

| Food (red) | Wine (blue) that balances it | Principle |
|---|---|---|
| Sweet tendency, fattiness | **Acidity / effervescence** | opposite |
| Juiciness (succulence), oiliness | **Alcoholicity / tannicity** | opposite |
| Saltiness, bitter tendency, sour tendency | **Softness**: sweetness, roundness | opposite |
| Sweetness (dessert) | **Sweetness** | parallel |
| T-O persistence, spiciness, aromaticity | **T-O intensity / persistence** | parallel |

When several components share an axis, plot the **leading** (highest) one. To complete a
wine for a dish, give each wine axis the score of the food it faces. To complete a dish
for a wine, do the reverse.

Reading two finished polygons:

| What you see | Reading |
|---|---|
| Same shape, low scores everywhere | Simple but **compatible** |
| Same shape, high scores everywhere | **Important** and well balanced |
| Different shape: the dish peaks where the wine is low | Simple but **incompatible** |
| Same shape, wine larger | **Wine overshadows food** |
| Same shape, food larger | **Food overshadows wine** |

## 2. True / false: what gets swapped

| Topic | The pair to know |
|---|---|
| Presenting | from the customer's **left**; pouring from the **right**, clockwise, ladies first |
| Serving order | coolest / least sweet first, sweet last; young before aged; Charmat › Crémant › Blanc de Blancs › Blanc de Noirs › Millésime › Demi-sec |
| Cellar | 11-15 °C, 65-70% humidity, bottles horizontal; sparkling at the **bottom**, important reds at the **top** |
| Gadgets | WINESAVE PRO and Coravin use **argon**; Üllo captures **sulfites**; eto keeps wine 2 weeks |
| Diseases | Fioretta and Acescenza are **aerobic**; Filante, Agrodolce, Girato, Amarore are **anaerobic** |
| Beer yeast | *S. carlsbergensis* **low** temperature, bottom (lager); *S. cerevisiae* **high** temperature, top (ale) |
| Spirits | Cognac: Charentais, double, Limousin oak; Armagnac: Armagnaçais, **continuous**, Gascogne; Calvados: Normandy, apple |
| Meat | white: veal, rabbit, lamb, chicken; red: beef, pork, horse; **black: game** |
