## The paper

**20 multiple-choice questions, one answer each.** The exam slide lists:

1. Macro & micronutrients basics
2. Supplementation and health (general questions)
3. Immunity & supplements
4. Gut health & probiotics
5. Supplementation and ageing
6. Small scenarios with questions to answer

Every distractor will be the **concept next door**: fat- vs water-soluble, pre- vs
postbiotic, innate vs adaptive, lifespan vs healthspan. Learn the pairs.

## 1. Macro & micronutrients

- **Macro = energy** (protein, carbs, fat); **micro = functions** (vitamins, minerals).
  Fat gives 9 kcal/g, protein and carbs 4.
- **Fat-soluble A, D, E, K** are stored, so they can reach toxicity. **Water-soluble B and C**
  are excreted and must be eaten regularly.

| Nutrient | Too little | Too much |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | **kwashiorkor**, edema, muscle wasting | kidney strain |
| Carbs | hypoglycaemic crash | insulin resistance, type 2 diabetes |
| Fat | dry skin, poor A/D/E/K absorption | high LDL, atherosclerosis, NAFLD |
| A | **night blindness** | liver damage |
| D | **rickets / osteomalacia** | **hypercalcaemia** |
| E | nerve damage (rare) | bleeding |
| K | bleeding | blocks **anticoagulants** |
| C | **scurvy** | diarrhoea |
| B1 / B3 / B9 / B12 | **beriberi** / **pellagra** / **neural tube defects** / **pernicious anaemia** | B6: nerve damage |
| Iron | **anaemia** | hemochromatosis |
| Iodine | **goitre** | thyroid dysfunction |
| Zinc | infections, stunting | blocks copper |
| Sodium / potassium | — / cramps, arrhythmia | hypertension / heart risk |

## 2. Supplementation and health

- **Supplement** adds nutrients to the diet. **Functional food** is a food with benefits
  beyond nutrition. **Nutraceutical** is a bioactive with clinical evidence. **Superfood**
  is marketing.
- The boom comes from **post-COVID awareness and ultra-processed diets**.
- The risks are toxicity, liver damage, **drug interactions**, and heavy metals.
- Evidence rule: **supplement to correct a deficiency**. Multivitamins are usually
  underdosed, so target D3, B12, magnesium and omega-3.
- **Formulation matters:** curcumin extract is **1%** bioavailable, **Meriva/liposome about 50×**.

## 3. Immunity & supplements

- **Innate** is fast, non-specific, with no memory (macrophages, **neutrophils**, the most
  abundant WBC). **Adaptive** is slow, specific, with **memory** (**B-cells make antibodies**,
  T-cells).
- **Balance:** too weak means infection; too strong means chronic inflammation or autoimmunity.
- **Acute** inflammation is short and protective. **Chronic** is low-grade and persistent,
  the thread through diabetes, CVD, obesity (fat secretes IL-6 and TNF-α) and depression.
- **About 70% of immune activity is in the gut.**
- **Evidence:** D, C and zinc are strong **for deficiency**; omega-3 is moderate; probiotics
  are strain-specific; curcumin is promising. **Vitamin D had the strongest COVID evidence**;
  vitamin C megadoses did not help.

## 4. Gut health & probiotics

- **Prebiotic** feeds (inulin, FOS, GOS, resistant starch). **Probiotic** is live, in
  adequate amounts, strain-identified. **Synbiotic** = both. **Postbiotic** is non-living
  (SCFAs, heat-killed cells) and stable.
- Probiotic effects are **strain-specific** and dose-dependent (CFU, 1–100 billion a day).
  The strongest evidence is **antibiotic-associated diarrhoea (−50%, S. boulardii, LGG)** and IBS.
- **Dysbiosis** is an imbalance, caused by antibiotics, low fibre, stress and poor sleep.
  **Leaky gut** means broken tight junctions.
- **Akkermansia** is low in obesity and diabetes. **GLP-1** slows gastric emptying and boosts
  insulin; stopping it brings back about two-thirds of the weight, and it costs muscle.

## 5. Supplementation & ageing

- **Healthspan** = years without chronic disease; lifespan = total years. The goal is
  morbidity compression.
- **12 hallmarks**, including telomere attrition, senescence (SASP), mitochondrial
  dysfunction, **inflammaging** and **dysbiosis**.
- **Antioxidants: balance, not elimination.** "More antioxidants = longer life" is
  exaggerated.
- NAD+ is 2–10% bioavailable orally, so the market uses **NMN/NR**. Oral **glutathione** is
  poorly absorbed and may feed tumours.
- **Lifestyle beats supplements**: diet gives 5–12 years, supplements 1–6. Blue Zones mean
  plants, movement and purpose.

## 6. Scenarios: the method

1. **Name the symptom cluster**, then the nutrient (bleeding gums means C; night blindness
   means A; goitre means iodine; pale and tired with heavy periods means iron; vegan with
   tingling means B12).
2. **Check for a drug**: warfarin means vitamin K; statins mean CoQ10; any herb means
   liver-enzyme interaction.
3. **Classify the product**: live, fibre, dead cells, or both? Supplement, functional food,
   or nutraceutical?
4. **Apply the evidence rule**: deficiency correction, a strain-specific claim, formulation,
   or lifestyle first.
