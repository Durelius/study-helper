## Foundations: the BPSE model

Mental health is emotional, psychological and social well-being, and the foundation for
learning. The **bio-psycho-socio-environmental** model splits it into four domains:

| Domain | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Biological**, the "hardware" | genetics, inflammation, microbiome, sleep, nutrition, hormones |
| **Psychological**, the "software" | beliefs, thinking patterns, emotional regulation, coping |
| **Social** | family, peers, culture, loneliness, stigma |
| **Environmental** | physical spaces, pollution, noise, urban stress, digital exposure, climate anxiety |

Stress and anxiety change three neurotransmitters:
- **noradrenaline** (energy, alertness, vigilance: "survival");
- **serotonin** (mood, impulse, sleep, appetite);
- **dopamine** (pleasure, reward, drive).

## Stress and cognition

**Acute stress** is a short cortisol rise that **sharpens focus** and is adaptive.
**Chronic stress** keeps cortisol high: it impairs the **prefrontal cortex**, **shrinks the
hippocampus**, and leads to brain fog, poor memory and burnout. **Allostatic load** is the
**cumulative wear** from chronic stress. Neuroinflammation driven by stress impairs
neurotransmitter synthesis and neurogenesis, hence anti-inflammatory nutrition.

## Brain nutrients

| Nutrient | Role / guideline |
|---|---|
| **Omega-3 DHA** | **97% of brain omega-3 is DHA**; oily fish 2×/week or **≥500 mg EPA + DHA a day**; helps mild cognitive impairment |
| **Folic acid, B12, B6** | lower **homocysteine** (linked to dementia); help MCI, **not** healthy older adults |
| Vitamin C | neurons hold high concentrations; antioxidant protection |
| **Vitamin D** | neuron growth and survival; low levels mean higher dementia risk; **D3 is more effective than D2** |
| Vitamin E, flavonoids | protect against decline, blood-vessel function |
| **Magnesium** | stress and sleep; **glycinate** relaxes and aids sleep, **L-threonate** helps mental clarity, **oxide** relieves constipation |

## The gut-brain axis

- **90–95% of serotonin** and **about 50% of dopamine** are made in the gut; bacteria also
  make GABA precursors.
- The **vagus nerve** is the highway, carrying more signals from gut to brain than the other way.
- The **enteric nervous system** is the "second brain".
- Stress causes dysbiosis, which drives neuroinflammation and worse mood: a vicious cycle.

**Psychobiotic strains:**

| Strain | Effect | Pathway |
|---|---|---|
| **L. rhamnosus JB-1** | less anxiety and cortisol | GABA, vagus |

| L. helveticus R0052 + B. longum R0175 | less anxiety and depression | |
| L. plantarum PS128 | better focus, less fatigue | dopamine and serotonin |
| B. breve A1 | cognitive support | BDNF |

## Diet and smart supplementation

**Diets:**
- the **Mediterranean** diet has strong evidence for depression prevention;
- the **MIND** diet targets brain health (leafy greens, berries, fish, nuts, whole grains);
- traditional Asian diets are protective, but urbanisation is eroding them.

**Evidence-based supplements:**
- **omega-3**, **magnesium**, **D3 + K2**;
- **L-theanine + caffeine** gives "calm alertness";
- **ashwagandha** (KSM-66) lowers cortisol and improves sleep;
- **lion's mane** stimulates **NGF**.

**Trends:** functional mushrooms, precision psychobiotics, mainstream adaptogens, nootropic
drinks. Class notes warn that many products are "hand waving" (ingredients are controlled,
not products), and that "brain-gut" drinks in 7-Eleven are often high in sugar.

## Youth mental health and the digital world

In Thailand, **1.5 million** people have depression and only **28%** seek treatment, with
stigma the main barrier. Students face TCAS exam stress, financial stress, loneliness and
noise. Campaigns and tools:
- DTac "Be OK";
- the Chula CUTC Wellness App;
- Into The Light Indonesia;
- UNICEF "Click Suk Jai".

Social media exploits the reward system through **variable-ratio reinforcement**, causing
doomscrolling, social comparison, sleep loss and fragmented attention. The fixes:
- **cognitive:** notice urges, set intentions;
- **environmental:** grayscale screens, notifications off, no phone in the bedroom;
- **behavioural:** replace scrolling with hobbies;
- **social:** agree group norms.

"If it's free, you are the product."
