## Why ageing matters

Ageing is not only a medical issue: it is also economic and urban-planning, and an
innovation opportunity. Life expectancy rose through **sanitation, vaccines, healthcare,
antibiotics and nutrition**. Societies now face ageing populations, more chronic disease,
dementia, rising costs, isolation and falling birth rates. Class notes add that COVID made
people see health and age as linked, and that "pets are the new kids", a growing
supplement market.

## Lifespan vs healthspan

- **Lifespan** = total years lived.
- **Healthspan** = years lived **without chronic disease**.
- The goal is **morbidity compression**: squeeze illness into the fewest final years.

**Biological ageing** is decline in function and rising disease vulnerability. It is
**modifiable**: about **70%** of the ageing rate is attributed to lifestyle and nutrition,
not genes. The ageing journey runs **prevent (30s) → maintain → mitigate decline (70s+)**
through diet, physical activity and sleep.

## The 12 hallmarks (López-Otín, Cell 2023)

| Hallmark | One line |
|---|---|
| Genomic instability | DNA damage accumulates, repair declines |
| **Telomere attrition** | telomeres **shorten with each division** |
| Epigenetic alterations | DNA methylation changes gene expression |
| Loss of proteostasis | protein quality control fails, misfolded proteins |
| **Disabled macroautophagy** | the cell's **recycling system** declines |
| **Deregulated nutrient sensing** | **mTOR** up, **AMPK/sirtuins** down |
| **Mitochondrial dysfunction** | **less ATP, more ROS** |
| **Cellular senescence** | "zombie" cells secrete **SASP**, ageing their neighbours |
| Stem cell exhaustion | repair and immune production fall |
| Altered intercellular communication | hormonal signalling declines |
| **Chronic inflammation (inflammaging)** | persistent low-grade inflammation driven by SASP |
| **Dysbiosis** | gut diversity falls, pathobionts rise |

The simplified classroom version has six: **oxidative stress, DNA damage, mitochondrial
dysfunction, cellular senescence, chronic inflammation, immune ageing**.

**Measuring biological age:**
- **Epigenetic clocks** (DNA methylation at CpG sites; Horvath, GrimAge) are the **most accurate**.
- **Telomere length** by PCR on blood.
- The **Levine PhenoAge** blood panel uses 9 standard tests (hsCRP, HbA1c, albumin…).

## Consequences of ageing

Ageing raises the risk of CVD, diabetes, osteoporosis, **sarcopenia** (muscle loss),
dementia and cancer.

| | Alzheimer's | Parkinson's |
|---|---|---|
| Hallmark pathology | **amyloid-beta plaques + tau tangles** | **loss of dopamine neurons**, alpha-synuclein |
| Note | begins **15–20 years** before symptoms | **gut-first hypothesis**: misfolding may start in the enteric nervous system |
| Affects | memory, reasoning, language, mood, behaviour | movement, sleep, mood, perception |

**Immunosenescence** is the age-related collapse of immune defence (thymic involution,
senescent cells). **Inflammaging** is the chronic low-grade inflammation that comes with it,
causing frailty, disease and cognitive decline. Class notes: iron given during a bacterial
infection can feed the bacteria.

## Oxidative stress: balance, not elimination

Cells naturally produce **reactive oxygen species** (superoxide, hydroxyl radical). In
excess they oxidise DNA, proteins and lipids; the brain is especially vulnerable. But at
**low levels ROS are essential signals** (Nrf2 defence, immune responses, exercise
adaptation: **hormesis**).

> **Exam focus.** "More antioxidants = longer life" is **scientifically incomplete and
> exaggerated**. The goal is **redox balance**, and **food-derived antioxidants beat
> high-dose isolated supplements**.

**Endogenous antioxidant systems:**

| System | Job |
|---|---|
| **SOD** (superoxide dismutase) | superoxide → H₂O₂; first-line mitochondrial defence |
| **Catalase** | H₂O₂ → water |
| **Glutathione (GSH)** | **master cellular antioxidant**, regenerates vitamins C and E |
| **Nrf2** | master regulator of 200+ antioxidant genes |
| **CoQ10** (ubiquinol) | mitochondrial electron transport, **depleted by statins** |
| **Melatonin** | **most potent endogenous antioxidant**, declines with age |

**Glutathione** is a big supplement business but has **poor oral bioavailability**
(degraded in the gut), which drives liposomal and IV forms. New research suggests it may
be **pro-tumour** in active cancer.

## Longevity nutrition and supplements

**Diets:** the **Mediterranean** pattern (vegetables, olive oil, legumes, fish, nuts,
moderate calories) is linked to lower inflammation, CV protection and cognition, unlike the
American diet (processed food, red meat, sugary drinks). Asian protective foods include
fermented foods, green tea, soy, seaweed and turmeric.

**Thai superfoods:**

| Food | Active |
|---|---|
| Butterfly pea (Dok Anchan) | **anthocyanins** |
| Mangosteen | **xanthones** |
| Holy basil (Kraprao) | rosmarinic acid, eugenol |
| Galangal | galangin |
| Turmeric | curcumin, an Nrf2 activator |
| Astaxanthin | 500–6,000× vitamin E |

**Tier 1 longevity supplements:**
- **Resveratrol:** SIRT1 and AMPK.
- **Spermidine:** the most potent natural **autophagy** inducer, mimics fasting.
- **Astaxanthin:** crosses the blood-brain barrier.
- **Quercetin:** a **senolytic** that clears senescent cells (with fisetin).

**NAD+.** Oral NAD+ is only **2–10% bioavailable**, so the market sells precursors:
- **NMN:** 250 mg/day raised blood NAD+ by about 38%; mainstream in Japan.
- **NR:** Tru Niagen.

**Other trends:**
- **collagen**, "beauty from within", huge in Thailand;
- **adaptogens** (ginseng, ashwagandha, reishi, rhodiola);
- **omega-3** for brain ageing (myelin is fat).

## Thailand and the region

Thailand is ageing fast:
- fertility has dropped to about 1.1;
- it is projected to be **super-aged by about 2035–2045**;
- there will be **12M+ elderly by 2033**;
- there will be under 2 workers per elderly person by 2050.

The crisis is concentrated in **rural areas**, which young people leave. The pressures are
healthcare, pensions, workforce and caregivers.

| Market | Trend |

## Blue Zones and lifestyle stacking

The Blue Zones share a **plant-dominant diet (about 95% plants)**, **natural daily
movement**, **purpose and social connection**, and stress management.

| Blue Zone | What stands out |
|---|---|
| Okinawa | the **moai** support network |
| Ikaria | olive oil |
| Sardinia | active herding |
| Nicoya | rest and family |

Shinrin-yoku (**forest bathing**) lowers blood pressure and cortisol.

**Lifestyle stacking** (biological-age reduction):

| Factor | Years |
|---|---|
| **Diet** | **5–12** |
| Exercise (150+ min/week plus strength) | 3–10 |
| Stress and social | 3–8 |
| Sleep (7–9 h) | 3–7 |
| Environment | 2–5 |
| **Supplements** | only **1–6** |

> **Exam focus.** Marketing hype vs reality: most supplements "may support health but do
> not stop ageing". The strongest evidence is **exercise, sleep, diet, no smoking, stress
> reduction and social connection**.
